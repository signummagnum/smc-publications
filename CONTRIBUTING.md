# Contribution and Curation Standard

The repository accepts only approved Signum Magnum College research-support materials, open educational resources, and metadata for genuine scholarly outputs.

Every contribution must:

1. identify its author or responsible institutional publisher;
2. state a version, publication date, and license;
3. distinguish verified facts, interpretation, and practical guidance;
4. cite primary or authoritative sources for regulatory and institutional claims;
5. avoid personal, learner, applicant, assessment, payment, or other confidential data;
6. avoid fabricated findings, metrics, reviews, testimonials, or research results;
7. avoid unsupported accreditation, recognition, ranking, salary, career, or outcome claims;
8. remain useful without advertising language or an enrollment call to action; and
9. respect copyright, research ethics, academic integrity, and applicable privacy requirements.

Drafts should be reviewed for factual accuracy, rights clearance, accessibility, and disclosure of material limitations before publication or deposit in an external repository.

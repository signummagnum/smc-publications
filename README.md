# Signum Magnum College — Public Research and Learning Resources

This repository is the public working collection for approved open research materials and open educational resources published by [Signum Magnum College](https://smceducation.com/).

Signum Magnum College is a Malta-based higher education institution operated by SMC Education Group Ltd. The College's institutional identity and verification routes are documented at [smceducation.com/institutional-identity](https://smceducation.com/institutional-identity/). Research services and approved institutional-output routes are described in the [SMC Research Lab](https://smceducation.com/research-portal/).

## Open educational resource

- [How to Verify an Online Degree: An Evidence-First Checklist](oer/online-degree-verification-checklist.md) — a practical, regulator-first workflow for prospective learners, advisers, employers, and researchers.

## Publication standard

Materials in this repository must be useful without an enrollment decision, must distinguish primary evidence from institutional claims, and must not contain personal or student data. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
